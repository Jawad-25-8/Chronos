ANCIENT EGYPT

One of the world's longest-lasting civilizations, developing along the Nile River for thousands of years.

LOCATION

Nile Valley

PERIOD

c. 3100 BCE – 30 BCE

KNOWN FOR

Pyramids & Pharaohs

WRITING

Hieroglyphs

OVERVIEW

A Civilization Built Along the Nile

Ancient Egypt developed around the Nile River in northeastern Africa. The river provided water, fertile farmland and an important route for transportation and trade.

Egyptian civilization became famous for its powerful pharaohs, monumental architecture, complex religious beliefs, writing system and advances in mathematics, engineering and medicine.

TIMELINE

Major Periods

01

Early Dynastic Period

Egypt became politically unified and the first dynasties established the foundations of the Egyptian state.

02

Old Kingdom

Often called the age of the pyramids, this period saw the construction of some of Egypt's greatest monuments.

03

Middle Kingdom

Egypt experienced political stability, economic growth and major developments in literature and administration.

04

New Kingdom

Egypt became a major regional power under rulers such as Hatshepsut, Akhenaten, Tutankhamun and Ramesses II.

ACHIEVEMENTS

What Did Egypt Leave Behind?

ARCHITECTURE

Pyramids, temples, tombs and massive monuments demonstrated remarkable engineering skills.

WRITING

Egyptians developed hieroglyphic writing and used written records for administration, religion and culture.

SCIENCE

Egyptian scholars developed practical knowledge of mathematics, astronomy, medicine and engineering.
