MESOPOTAMIA

The ancient land between the Tigris and Euphrates Rivers, where some of the world's earliest cities and civilizations emerged.

LOCATION

Fertile Crescent

PERIOD

c. 6000 BCE – 539 BCE

FAMOUS FOR

Cities & Writing

WRITING

Cuneiform

OVERVIEW

Where Cities Began

Mesopotamia was a region located between the Tigris and Euphrates Rivers, roughly corresponding to parts of modern-day Iraq, Syria and Turkey.

The fertile land allowed agriculture to flourish. Over time, villages grew into cities, cities became city-states and powerful kingdoms emerged.

Mesopotamia was not one single empire. Instead, many different peoples and states rose and fell across the region over thousands of years.

THE BIG PLAYERS

Who Ruled Mesopotamia?

01

Sumerians

One of the earliest urban civilizations. Sumerian city-states such as Uruk and Ur became major centers of culture and trade.

02

Akkadian Empire

Founded by Sargon of Akkad, it became one of the earliest known territorial empires.

03

Babylon

Babylon became a major political and cultural center. King Hammurabi is famous for his influential law code.

04

Assyria

The Assyrians created a powerful empire and developed highly organized military and administrative systems.

WHAT DID THEY CREATE?

Ideas That Changed Humanity

✍️ CUNEIFORM

One of the world's earliest known writing systems, originally used to record things such as goods, transactions and administration.

🏙️ CITIES

Mesopotamian societies built some of the earliest large cities, including Uruk, Ur and Babylon.

⚖️ LAW

Hammurabi's Code is one of the best-known surviving ancient collections of laws.

🌾 AGRICULTURE

Irrigation systems helped farmers use river water to grow crops and support growing cities.

🤯 WAIT... WHAT?

They Had Homework Too.

Archaeologists have discovered ancient school texts written on clay tablets. Some contain mathematical exercises and writing practice made by students thousands of years ago.
